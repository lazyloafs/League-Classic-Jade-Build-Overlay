# Multi-objective builds: defense vs DPS vs gold

Each archetype is a full **6-item** build. A multi-objective search looked for the Pareto front of three goals at once: **maximize defense**, **maximize DPS**, **minimize gold cost**. A build is on the front when no other 6-item build is at least as good on all three and better on one. Generated by `tools/pareto.js` (population 300, 400 generations, 3 seeds merged, about 536 front points per archetype).

**How to read the tables.** Every row is a Pareto point: no 6-item build beats it on defense and DPS without costing more gold. "Balance" means defense x DPS, each scaled 0-1 against that archetype's best on the front. "Defense floor" rows give the most DPS that keeps at least that share of the archetype's best defense.

## Assumptions

- Base stats before items (level 18): AD build {"hp":1800,"armor":70,"mr":45,"ad":100,"as":0.7}, AP build {"hp":1700,"armor":60,"mr":40}, both tanks {"hp":2100,"armor":100,"mr":50,"ad":80,"as":0.65}. DPS targets a 2500 HP, 100 armor, 60 MR enemy.
- Same item rules as the first report: finished items only, one boots, one Sunfire, one jungle item. Guardian Angel counts as +30% max HP and Maw of Malmortius as a 400 magic shield. Stacking items use their base stats.
- Actives, spellblade procs and auras that help allies are not counted. Guessed numbers: Malady 15 on-hit, Statikk Shiv 100 per 5 attacks, Blade of the Ruined King at 60% target HP.
- Check: the max-defense and max-DPS ends of the fronts match the exhaustive search in the first report (AD DPS 921, armor tank 28,902, MR tank 22,338).
