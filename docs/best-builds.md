## Assumptions

- Tank at level 18 before items: 2100 HP, 100 armor, 50 MR. Score = effective HP = HP x (1 + resist/100). Guardian Angel counts as +30% max HP; Maw of Malmortius as a 400 magic shield; Leviathan's 15% reduction only when stacked.
- Carry at level 18 before items: 100 AD, 0.7 attack speed, 1800 HP, 500 mana. Target: 2500 HP, 100 armor, 60 MR. Attack speed capped at 2.5.
- DPS = sustained auto-attack damage per second: attack damage, attack speed, crit (200% crits, 250% with Infinity Edge), armor penetration (Last Whisper 35%, Black Cleaver 25% reduction + 10 lethality, Youmuu's 20 lethality) and on-hit damage. Spellblades, spell damage and item actives are not counted.
- Values not in the item text and assumed: Malady 15 magic on-hit, Statikk Shiv 100 magic per 5 attacks, Blade of the Ruined King at 60% target HP, Guinsoo's at 8 stacks, Sword of the Divine passive 45% attack speed.
